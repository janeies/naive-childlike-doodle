# Prompt Recipes

Adapt these structures to the user's subject. Do not copy placeholders into the final prompt.

## Original illustration

Describe the subject, pose, and emotional beat first. Then specify irregular near-black hand-drawn contours, simplified broad shapes, mild asymmetry, sparse interior detail, warm off-white paper, selective gray halftone, and no more than two muted accent colors. Define two to five scene cues and preserve generous negative space. Exclude smooth vectors, photorealism, glossy 3D, gradients, decorative clutter, and unwanted text.

## Reference-preserving redraw

Use the attached image as the structural reference. Preserve the exact subject count, pose, expression, silhouette, distinctive facial marks, accessories, object relationships, and overall framing. Redraw only the rendering language: irregular childlike black lines, simplified secondary details, relaxed perspective, warm off-white negative space, selective gray halftone, and restrained muted accents. Do not redesign the character or replace products with generic alternatives.

## Simplify an existing scene

Keep the main subject and the minimum environmental cues needed to identify the place. Remove roughly half of the secondary lines, repeated textures, tiny architecture, and background clutter. Make long lines visibly imperfect. Replace broad gray fills with sparse halftone only where they describe planes or shadows. Preserve the original composition and emotional tone.

## Targeted edit

Change only [named region/property]. Preserve every other object, contour, character feature, color, texture, crop, and spatial relationship. Match the new marks to the existing irregular line weight and handmade texture. Do not regenerate or reinterpret unaffected areas.

## Transparent object

Isolate [object] and output it as a single PNG with true alpha transparency. Preserve the object's real shape, handmade texture, colors, openings, handles, closures, motifs, and small construction details. Reconstruct only the explicitly requested missing or occluded portion. Include no person, room, floor, background, border, text, or baked-in checkerboard. Output separate files when multiple objects are requested.

## Character grounding correction

Keep the character and scene unchanged. Add only a small irregular contact shadow or sparse gray halftone directly beneath the feet/body, consistent with the scene's light direction and naive drawing style. If requested, lower the character brightness slightly without altering the line art, palette identity, expression, or proportions.
